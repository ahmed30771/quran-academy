Get started

A short tutorial before you enroll

Click through the four cards. This is the same path we use on day one.

Create your account

Choose Student or Teacher on the login page. Students can register for classes; teachers apply to teach. Admin is a separate visual login for academy staff.

Pick a course

Kids Tajweed, adult Nazra, Hifz, or Quranic Arabic. Filter by track on the Courses page. Fees are sample amounts you can replace later.

Meet your teacher

After a free trial or placement chat, you are paired with a certified teacher. You can message them on WhatsApp from your dashboard.

Your first class

Arrive five minutes early, keep a Quran nearby, and follow the teacher’s on-screen mushaf. Homework appears in the student dashboard after class.

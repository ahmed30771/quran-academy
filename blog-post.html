Five Tajweed habits that stick at home

This is a sample article for the academy journal. It is not a fatwa and not a substitute for a teacher. The aim is a calm home rhythm so class time is not the only time letters are heard.

1. One letter, not a page

After class, repeat only the letter or rule the teacher marked. Five clean repeats are kinder than racing through a page with the same mistake.

2. Hear it before saying it

Play a short recitation of the ayah, then pause. Children often copy melody before they copy makhraj — a teacher still needs to check, but the ear is warming up.

3. Keep the mushaf in the same place

A dedicated corner — even a small one — tells the household this is recitation time, not leftover homework time.

4. Praise the correction, not the speed

“That qaf was clearer” lands better than “finish two pages.” Speed comes after the letter is stable.

5. Stop while it is still pleasant

End on a successful line. The next sitting starts easier when the last memory is not frustration.
